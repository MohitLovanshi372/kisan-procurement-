⚠️ The Problem We Address

Farmers often face long waiting times, lack of information about procurement schedules, and uncertainty about procurement and payment status.

🎯 Our Mission

To make the agricultural procurement journey simple, transparent and organized by giving farmers easy access to schedules, digital tokens, queue information and procurement status.

💡 Our Solution

Mandisathi is a farmer-centric digital platform that brings procurement scheduling, digital token generation, queue information, procurement tracking and payment status into one simple workflow.

💻 Technologies Used & Problem-Solution Architecture

Every technology in Mandisathi is engineered to solve a specific, high-friction pain point faced by farmers and procurement centers during the MSP season.

⚡
Node.js & Express.js
Backend REST Microservice
⚠️ Problem
Manual paper slips, fragmented records, and system crashes during high-volume harvest arrivals across state mandi centers.
✅ Solution
Asynchronous non-blocking event loop handles thousands of concurrent farmer requests, instant token booking, and synchronized center status in milliseconds.
📡
Socket.io (WebSockets)
Bi-Directional Real-Time Engine
⚠️ Problem
Farmers waiting 8–14 hours at mandi gates with no visibility into queue movement, causing severe physical congestion and tractor jams.
✅ Solution
Live event streaming (`queue-update`, `token:status_changed`) broadcasts real-time token numbers, estimated wait countdowns, and turn notifications directly to phones.
📱
Digital QR & Security Tokens
Gate Pass & Anti-Fraud Verification
⚠️ Problem
Middleman impersonation, duplicate paper slip forgeries, and slow manual handwriting entry at weighbridge checkpoints.
✅ Solution
Instant cryptographic dynamic QR codes allow rapid 3-second contactless scan at gate, weighing scale, and quality lab, blocking unauthorized middleman access.
🗄️
Persistent Multi-Model Store
Audit Trail & Transaction Ledger
⚠️ Problem
Lost procurement slips, weight manipulation disputes, and lack of verifiable audit history between procurement agencies and farmers.
✅ Solution
Atomic multi-model data schemas maintain unalterable timestamps for every workflow transition (Booked → Weighed → Inspected → Settled) with complete transparency.
🌐
Vernacular i18n Engine
Bilingual Accessibility (हिन्दी/English)
⚠️ Problem
Complex English-only portals and confusing bureaucratic terms create an insurmountable digital barrier for grassroots rural farmers.
✅ Solution
Zero-latency bilingual switching with simplified agricultural terminology, clear iconography, and voice-assisted readiness designed for rural smartphone users.
💰
Direct MSP & DBT Tracker
Zero-Commission Payment Pipeline
⚠️ Problem
Middlemen charging unauthorized commissions (आढ़त), distress selling below MSP rates, and delayed unverified payments.
✅ Solution
Guaranteed formula enforcing official MSP benchmarks (₹2,275/Qtl) directly calculating payout amounts with bank account validation and transaction UTR tracking.
📱
Mobile-First UI Architecture
Accessible Responsive Web Design
⚠️ Problem
Heavy desktop software that fails on slow 2G/3G rural networks and is difficult to use under direct outdoor sunlight on fields.
✅ Solution
Ultra-fast glassmorphic stylesheet with high-contrast sunlight readability, 48px touch targets for rugged fingers, and minimal data consumption.
